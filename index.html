06 · How we work

Five steps. No surprises.

Fixed scope, fixed price, named rounds. You always know which step you are on and what lands next.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    One call. We learn the market, the customer, and what the brand has to beat.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Scope, timeline, and deliverables written down before any design starts.

  3. 03

    Design & Development

    The system gets built: marks, palette, type, and the templates that carry them.

  4. 04

    Revisions

    Named rounds against the brief. Feedback in one place, changes in one pass.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Every file, every format, documented. You own it outright on handover.
